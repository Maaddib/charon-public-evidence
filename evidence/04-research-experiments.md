# H04 - Research experimentation

**Period:** April-June 2026  
**Evidence:** dated local scripts, research protocol and generated outputs

## Research problem

Test whether discourse signals could support functional classification of
digital-fraud ecosystems while maintaining non-attribution and privacy
controls.

## Contemporaneous technical evidence

The working directory records successive experiments in:

- Reddit collection and signal scanning;
- adaptive phrase and candidate discovery;
- topic and moderation-pattern analysis;
- TF-IDF term analysis;
- supervised classification;
- entity extraction; and
- coded-dataset validation and repair.

The preserved research protocol defined purposive stratified sampling and a
collection window from January 2025 to June 2026. Its ethics controls required
public discourse only, no interaction or deception, no private messages, no
attempts to identify offenders, removal or hashing of usernames, exclusion of
direct identifiers, and aggregate or function-level reporting.

## What was learned

This stage established useful analytical experiments but also highlighted the
difference between research classification and defensible investigative
findings. Later Charon architecture therefore separated raw evidence,
observations, entity mentions, resolved entities, relationships, assessment
and analysis.

## Evidential caution

Most of this stage was preserved as working scripts and outputs in a later Git
snapshot. File dates support the sequence, but they are weaker evidence than
contemporaneous individual commits. The public repository states that
limitation explicitly and does not publish the underlying corpus.
