# H02 - Research-led EDD architecture

**Date:** 15 December 2025  
**Primary commit:** `05a3561`  
**Recorded change:** 40 files and 2,530 inserted lines

## Research and design problem

Move beyond a single exploratory case into a repeatable investigation method
that separated case inputs from reusable analytical logic.

## Contemporaneous technical evidence

The commit added a profile manifest, nine ordered investigation stages, shared
public-source packs, a case template, validation tools and a structured report
template. The stages covered identity, footprint, corporate records,
litigation, reputation, behaviour, networks, property signals and risk
scoring.

The profile also recorded boundaries that later became central to Charon:

- public-source collection only;
- data minimisation and purpose limitation;
- auditability, fairness and context;
- allegations distinguished from proven outcomes;
- inferences explicitly labelled;
- paid checks disabled unless authorised; and
- corroboration required where identity-collision risk was high.

## What changed

The project moved from configurable collection into an explicit methodology:
cases supplied inputs, while reusable profiles defined research and assessment
logic.

## Limitation and next step

The manifest described intended workflow and outputs, but the runner and full
evidence chain were not yet the mature, persistent system seen later in 2026.
